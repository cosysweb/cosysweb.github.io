Service Rates and Terms

 Services Provided.
COSYS will provide the following services (the “Services”) to the Client under the "COSYS Service Agreement".
      a. COSYS agrees to provide consulting services, as requested by Client, related to computer hardware, software, network configuration, operating systems and networks, database development, programming, and other similar work. Such work will be performed by COSYS on behalf of Client and will be performed at a location or locations to be determined by COSYS. For example, services might include installing and configuring Windows operating systems, Internet servers, and associated software.
       b. Regular Consulting Hours “Regular Consulting Hours” shall consist of any time COSYS works for Client during regular business hours. Regular business hours are defined as 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST Monday through Friday excluding national holidays.
       c. Extended Consulting Hours “Extended Consulting Hours” shall consist of any hours worked by COSYS outside of the period defined as Regular Consulting Hours. This includes weekends, the period 5:00 PM to 9:00 AM EST during weekdays, and all holidays.

 Additional Work.
  After receipt of an order that adds to the Services, COSYS may take reasonable action and expend reasonable amounts of time and money based on such order. Client shall not be required to pay any extra charges for additional work unless such work and the charges there for have been approved by Client in advance.
 Client agrees to pay COSYS for such action and expenditure as set forth in the "COSYS Service Agreement". Furthermore, Client agrees to pay COSYS for any required materials or client requested hardware, software, and contacted outside vendors.

 Cost of Services.
  (a) All Regular Hours Rates, as defined in the "COSYS Service Agreement", will be provided by COSYS at a rate of one hundred sixty-five dollars ($165.00) per hour.
 (b) All Extended Consulting Hours, as defined in the "COSYS Service Agreement", will be provided by COSYS at a rate of three hundred thirty dollars ($330.00) per hour. COSYS and Client hereby agree that the requirements for scheduling Extended Consulting Hours will be on a mutually agreeable basis.
 (c) Service Calls requesting Urgent / RUSH service. If Client requests Urgent / Rush service COSYS will do their best to rearrange schedules to try to accommodate Client at an additional charge of $100.00 per Urgent / RUSH service request.
 (d) All onsite services charges include time for "point-to-location" time. The service ticket billing starts when a COSYS technicians leaves the current location or COSYS office.

 Terms.
   (a)  All Hardware, Software, and Contracted Outside Vendors will be pre-paid by the Client prior to COSYS ordering. All Hardware and Software remains the property of the COSYS until paid in full.
   (b)  All Invoices to Client shall be “Payment Due Upon Receipt”. Invoices will be email to email address on file or delivered by hand.
   (c)  Any unpaid sums over 10 days old that are not in dispute shall bear interest at the rate of 1.5 percent per month in additional to a twenty-five dollar ($25.00) late fee until paid in full. Costs of collection for such undisputed amounts, including reasonable attorney's fees, shall be borne by the Client.
  (d)  There is a two hour minimum charge for all onsite visits for services not covered by a separate “Agreement for Shared Vision Services” attachment. There is a one hour minimum charge for remote support services not covered by a separate “Agreement for Shared Vision Services” attachment.
