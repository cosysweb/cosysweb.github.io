Shared Vision Plans

Shared Vision is the service provided by COSYS - Systems Solutions Group for your new outsourced IT department.

COSYS offers two levels of Shared Vision. A limited IT support service called Shared Vision - Gold. Shared Vision Gold includes security software and service, software patching service, and access to support. Also included is monitoring and reporting on the your IT assets. Labor for maintaining the most important IT asset, your servers, is also included.

COSYS offers an all inclusive IT support service called Shared Vision - Platinum. Shared Vision - Platinum covers everything in the Gold plan with the addition of all IT support labor at one fixed monthly cost. Whether it be remote support or onsite support, all IT support is included in Shared Vision Platinum plan. Unlimited IT support! All IT support labor is included except for training and project labor. That gives the business one fixed cost for IT services for the year! No surprises!

Our Guarantee

We are so confident in the Shared Vision Platinum program that we are willing to back it up with a powerful guarantee that no other IT support company can make.

Under the Shared Vision Platinum plan we guarantee that we will be able to detect, diagnose, and prevent any type of network problem from escalating into downtime. If by some odd chance your network goes down, if you get a virus, a hacker invasion, or any other problem that requires clean up and fixing, we will do all of the labor necessary to restore your network back to full health with no additional labor fees to your business.
